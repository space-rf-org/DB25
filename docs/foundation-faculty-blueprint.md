# Attracting Distinguished Faculty — a Structural Blueprint

**Position.** Faculty at CMU, TUM, Berkeley, Stanford, MIT, and Caltech cannot be
bought; they can be *equipped*. They come where the instrument, the artifact, and the
open problem already exist. We have all three — `srf-db` and `DB25` are running code
with named unsolved problems — so recruitment is a packaging problem, not a funding one.

**The lure: paper-sized problems, not a job description.**

- Mechanised refinement from the ChronoQuorum TLA+ specs to the C23 core (ADR-0003 F3).
- Optimality of the settling window `W_eff` under bounded gPTP error, with
  halt-on-divergence as a *specified* terminal state.
- Deterministic sensor fusion over a totally ordered log — deleting the data-incest
  apparatus, not solving it.
- Alloy-checked equivalence of subquery decorrelation under bag and three-valued
  semantics — DB25’s riskiest rewrite.
- Falsifiability as a test-suite metric: the `db25_gate` flags vacuous tests and
  surfaced four genuine optimizer bugs on its first run.
- Spec-before-code TLA+ for the executor, MVCC, and recovery layers not yet built.

**Five structural commitments.**

1. **Affiliate Fellows, not employees.** One day a week, a named chair, no relocation,
   university appointment retained.
2. **Students are the bandwidth.** Each Fellow receives funded PhD and postdoc lines;
   the Fellow supervises, the foundation pays.
3. **Publication-first IP.** The foundation takes a non-exclusive licence; Fellows
   publish freely after a 30-day review; artifacts open by default. This is the clause
   technology-transfer offices will sign.
4. **The testbed is the draw.** A TSN cluster on i.MX8M+/i.MX95, the benchmark corpus,
   the TLA+ models, and the end-to-end harness, offered as a shared,
   artifact-evaluation-ready instrument no single lab can stand up.
5. **Commercial funnel behind a firewall.** For-profit subsidiaries license foundation
   IP at arm's length and endow the fellowships; Fellows hold advisory equity, never
   contract deliverables.

**First move.** Send the problems as co-authorship invitations.
